Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy applies to Trillium Sense, the iPhone, iPad, and Mac app published by an independent developer as part of Ambient Lab, a project of M-of-N. Trillium Sense is the App Store name for the Trillium product. It does not cover the m-of-n.com website, which has a separate privacy notice.

The short version: Trillium Sense does not collect personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing that identifies you. Trillium Sense has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not contact any network servers, and it does not send information to M-of-N or to any other party.

How the app communicates

The app talks only to a Trillium board that you connect, and to other music software on the same device. The connection uses USB-MIDI or, with a battery-powered board, Bluetooth MIDI (BLE-MIDI). On Apple devices the app uses CoreMIDI so your board can appear as a standard MIDI instrument. These connections stay on your device and your board. They are not sent over the internet.

What is stored on your device

Settings, presets, and firmware backups are stored locally on your device. On a Mac, that data is kept in the app’s Application Support folder. On iPhone and iPad, it is kept in the app’s container. This data is not uploaded anywhere. You can remove it by deleting the app and, on a Mac, its Application Support folder.

Firmware

Firmware for the Trillium board is bundled in the app. If you install or restore firmware, the transfer is made directly to your board over the local connection. We do not receive a copy of your firmware or of any backup you make.

Microphone and audio

The microphone is on the Trillium board, not in the app, and the app does not use your device’s own microphone. For testing only, the Mac app has a capture switch and a play-through switch, both off whenever the app starts. They show a chart of the board’s microphone audio and can play it through your Mac’s speakers. That audio stays on your device. Nothing is recorded or saved, and nothing is sent to M-of-N or to any server.

Bluetooth

The app asks for Bluetooth permission only so you can pair your own Trillium board as a Bluetooth MIDI instrument, using the system’s Bluetooth MIDI panel. The app does not scan for or connect to other devices, does not use Bluetooth to track you, and collects no data over Bluetooth. Your board’s battery level and Bluetooth settings are read over the USB cable and stay on your device.

Children’s privacy

Trillium Sense does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Links to retailers

Some links on the Trillium web pages to retailers are affiliate links. Following one takes you to the retailer, whose own cookies and privacy policy apply. The Trillium web pages set no tracking cookies and run no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
