Trillium

New ways to make music, together.

Trillium is an emerging music company. We are building an app that opens new ways for people to create, play and interact through music.

It starts simply. Trillium Zero is one small microcontroller board with a built-in motion sensor on a USB cable. Move, tilt or tap it, and it plays GarageBand, Logic, AUM or any MIDI app as a standard USB MIDI instrument. The Trillium app chooses the instrument, shows the sensors live and installs the firmware.

Status: early and experimental. Trillium 0.7.0 carries firmware 0.7.0 and installs it on a Trillium Zero from the macOS app. The iPhone app has not yet run on hardware.
