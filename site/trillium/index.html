Trillium

A companion app for motion- and touch-sensing musical instruments.

Trillium monitors and configures instruments built around a Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Sense and its onboard IMU. Several hardware models are planned, from a motion-only board to one with a Bela Trill Flex touch sensor; see Hardware below. The instruments play as class-compliant USB MIDI devices into GarageBand, Logic, AUM, or any MIDI app. The app provides presets and sensor modes such as touch, tilt scale, and theremin.

Status: early and experimental. Trillium 0.6.0 carries firmware 0.6.0 and installs it on a Trillium Zero from the macOS app. The iPhone app has not yet run on hardware.
