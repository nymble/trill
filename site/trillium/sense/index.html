Trillium Sense

The companion app. Free.

Choose an instrument, map motion to MIDI and keep the firmware current on Trillium Zero. Works with any MIDI app or synth.

Features

  • Presets Ten built-in instruments. Edit, save, duplicate, export.
  • Mapping Notes, scale, range, controllers, taps, strikes, gestures. Edits play live.
  • Sensors Live motion charts, board pose, tilt zones, strike compass, Linear sensor touches.
  • Instrument view Handpan (prototype): strike in 8 directions, tilt for a second ring and the ding.
  • Bluetooth MIDI Pair a battery-powered board through the system panel. Settings and sensor data stay on USB.
  • MIDI monitor Every message the instrument sends.
  • Firmware Install and update from the app, with a backup first (macOS).
